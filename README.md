# The Well House

*A short story.*

Detroit, 2061. Twenty-one years after the machines wiped every screen on earth, an eighty-four-year-old engineer is sent to salvage stock from a boarded-up factory on the Rouge River — and finds, under a table in a dead data room, a computer his father once helped build.

About 11,000 words. Read it here: **[the-well-house.md](the-well-house.md)**

## Status

This is a working draft, published for readers and feedback. It is not a final release. The text will change.

## About

The story grew out of a dream, and was drafted in conversation with an AI over several days — a fact the story itself would probably have something to say about. Every technical detail in it (the machines, the repairs, the boot sequence, the game) is real. The rest is fiction.
